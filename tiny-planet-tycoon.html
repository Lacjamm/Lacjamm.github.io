How to Play

The improved loop is faster, clearer, and more chaotic.
1. Click for combos👆
Click the planet quickly to build a combo multiplier. Letting the timer expire resets it.
2. Buy upgrades🏗️
Upgrades add idle income, people, chaos, defenses, and new things on the planet.
3. Trigger Disaster Rush☄️
When chaos charges the meter, start a meteor mini-game. Click meteors to earn rewards and protect your population.
4. Prestige✨
Destroying the planet gives Star Dust, which permanently boosts every future run.
