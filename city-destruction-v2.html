Bring the skyline down.

This is an original 2D destruction sandbox inspired by the feel of city-smashing games: a dense multi-district map, screen-filling disasters, dynamic debris, fires, traffic, and cinematic effects. Swap between Cataclysm, Creature, and Futuristic genres, fine-tune each power with its own size and strength sliders, call in a bomber-delivered nuke, unleash toxic and cryo storms, summon a horror-style Blood Moon eclipse, breach the streets with an Abyss Leviathan, shred towers with a cyber-tech Nanite Bloom, chain one-way Portal Rift exits that can suspend anything they swallow in a holding dimension, call sandworms, sky dragons, alien saucers, hunter-drone swarms, giant creatures, and reworked amber-cored tripods, fire a blue-white Satellite Cannon that blooms into a cinematic dome blast, or bring in a Death Star strike that burns the whole skyline down. Kaiju Rampage and Titan Ape now hunt rival giant monsters too, and Esc opens a pause menu with keybind editing, UI scaling, a slow-motion toggle, a stronger multi-level stylized blood FX setting, resume, and quit-to-title controls. Traffic, civilian boundaries, hitboxes, train destruction, and tornado visuals have all been polished.
